I’m an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student at [KAIST AI](https://gsai.kaist.ac.kr/?lang=en), advised by [Hyunwoo Kim](https://hyunw.kim/), and a research intern at [NVIDIA Research](https://www.nvidia.com/).

My research aims to understand how models can generate data for other models and learn from it while preserving its quality and diversity. My work centers on two directions:

1. Data Synthesis and Selection with LLMs: I study what makes training data good and how to produce it at scale with minimal human effort. My work spans data selection, targeted synthesis, distillation, and inference-time interaction.
2. Diversity Preservation in LLMs: I study when and why LLMs lose diversity, and how to preserve it through the data they are trained on, with a focus on settings where diversity matters most, such as RL exploration and non-verifiable tasks.

Previously, I interned on the coding agent team at [Upstage](https://www.upstage.ai/), where I contributed to Korea's national Sovereign AI project. I received my B.S. in computer science from [Korea University](https://www.korea.edu/).
