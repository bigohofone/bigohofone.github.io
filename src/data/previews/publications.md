My current research focuses on building self-improving machines. For any learner, human or machine, what to learn from matters, but who makes that call matters more. People become truly capable once they start steering their own learning, seeking out information, practicing what they struggle with, and adapting when things get unfamiliar. A model that can do the same isn’t limited to what humans can curate or anticipate in advance.

I’m especially excited about:

(1) Model-driven data synthesis and selection: models that acquire, create, select, and structure their own learning experiences with minimal human supervision, much like a student who no longer needs a teacher.

(2) Test-time environment internalization: models that, given a task in an unfamiliar environment, actively explore to figure out what they need to know, distill what they find into reusable knowledge, and use it to act effectively, just as people do.
