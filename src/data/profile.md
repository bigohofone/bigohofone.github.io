Hello, I’m an integrated M.S./Ph.D. student at [KAIST AI](https://gsai.kaist.ac.kr/?lang=en), advised by [Hyunwoo Kim](https://hyunw.kim/).

My research aims to build machines that learn autonomously. For any learner, what it learns from matters, but who decides what it learns from is the more fundamental question, one of agency as much as of method. The capacity to direct one’s own mind, to seek out knowledge, and to adapt to the unfamiliar is what has allowed humanity to endure across generations of upheaval.

This motivates two directions in my current work: (1) model-driven data synthesis and selection, in which models gather, generate, curate, and organize their own learning experiences with minimal human oversight; and (2) test-time environment internalization, in which models placed in an unfamiliar environment actively explore to determine what they need to know, distill their findings into reusable knowledge, and apply it to act effectively.

Previously, I interned at [Upstage](https://www.upstage.ai/), where I contributed to their Sovereign AI project. Working within the coding agent team, I helped develop the Solar Open2 and Solar Pro4 models. I received my B.S. in computer science from [Korea University](https://www.korea.edu/).
